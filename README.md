# 🎓 EduQuest AI
### Your AI Teacher. Your Learning World. Your Adventure.

EduQuest AI is an AI-powered, gamified learning platform that transforms traditional study materials into personalized, interactive learning experiences.

Instead of simply reading notes and memorizing information, students can upload their study materials, interact with an intelligent tutor, take personalized quizzes, identify misconceptions, complete learning quests, and track their academic progress.

Our mission is to make learning more engaging, personalized, measurable, and effective.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📚 1. Smart Study Material Processing
- Upload PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and TXT files.
- Extract and organize study content.
- Automatically identify important topics.
- Organize learning materials into structured knowledge units.
- Track document processing status.

### 🤖 2. AI-Powered Tutor
- Ask questions based on uploaded study materials.
- Receive contextual explanations.
- Retrieve relevant information from documents.
- View supporting citations and source references.
- Ask follow-up questions.
- Avoid unsupported answers through evidence-grounded retrieval.

### 📝 3. Intelligent Diagnostic Quizzes
- Generate topic-based quizzes.
- Support multiple-choice, true/false, and short-answer questions.
- Evaluate submitted answers.
- Provide explanations and feedback.
- Identify weak topics and knowledge gaps.
- Track quiz performance.

### 🧠 4. Misconception Detection
- Identify recurring learning mistakes.
- Detect potential misconceptions.
- Verify misunderstandings through follow-up questions.
- Generate corrective mini-lessons.
- Track improvement and resolution.

### 🌍 5. Gamified Learning Worlds
- Convert study topics into learning worlds.
- Complete topic-based quests.
- Unlock challenges.
- Fight educational boss battles.
- Progress through learning levels.

### ⭐ 6. XP and Achievement System
- Earn XP by completing learning activities.
- Unlock achievements.
- Track learning streaks.
- Progress through levels.
- Monitor learning milestones.

### 📊 7. Learning Analytics
- Track academic progress.
- Monitor quiz performance.
- Identify weak subjects and topics.
- Visualize learning activity.
- Monitor XP and achievement progression.

## 🧠 What Makes EduQuest AI Different?

Traditional learning platforms primarily focus on delivering educational content.

EduQuest AI focuses on understanding how students learn.

It connects multiple learning activities into one continuous feedback loop:

**Upload → Understand → Ask → Practice → Detect → Improve → Achieve**

The platform doesn't just help students find answers. It aims to identify why they struggle and guide them towards understanding.

---

## ⚙️ Initial MVP Architecture

The initial MVP is designed to operate without requiring a separate Gemini API key.

The RuleBasedProvider provides:
- Keyword-based document retrieval.
- Topic extraction.
- Evidence-grounded responses.
- Quiz generation.
- Quiz evaluation.
- Misconception workflows.
- Quest and learning recommendations.

Gemini integration is planned as a future enhancement through the modular AI provider architecture.
